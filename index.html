Variable Syntax Guide

Advanced Variable Features:
  • Recursive Scaling: You can embed variables inside variables. Example: Define TOTAL_MOD as [STR]+[PROF]. Then, simply use 1d20+[TOTAL_MOD] in your roll buttons.
  • Order of Operations: The engine resolves variables first, then calculates the math string. Keep formulas clean by nesting simple variables into complex ones.

Button Formula Syntax Guide

  • Standard Syntax: 1d20+4 or 1d20+[PROF]+[DEX]
  • Complex Multiplication: [PROF]*(1d10) or ([STR]*2)+1d4
  • Variable Dice: [PROF]d10
  • Advantage: 2d20kh1+[STR] (Keep Highest 1)
  • Disadvantage: 2d20kl1+[STR] (Keep Lowest 1)
  • Savage Attacker: 2d12p2kh1+[STR] (Pool 2 times, Keep Highest 1 total)
  • Daggerheart Check: 2d12daggerheart+2
  • Binary Check (Failure/Success): 1d20+[STR]vs[TARGET_DC]
  • Roll Under Check (Success/Fail): 1d100lessthanvs[SKILL]
  • Ternary Check (Failure/Mixed/Strong): 2d6+[WIS]vs7/10
  • Dynamic DCs: 1d20+[STR]vs[ENEMY_AC]/[ENEMY_AC]+5
  • Explosive Dice (Aces): 3d6explosive(reroll any dice that lands on highest number and keep adding)
  • Critical Modifiers: Append modifiers right after a dice roll to evaluate crits without affecting the numeric math. Available triggers: max, min, doubles, yahtzee, ranges like 19-20, or character variables.
    Examples: 1d100critsuccess[min]critfail[max]lessthanvs[TARGET_DC] 1d100critsuccess[min]critfail[max]lessthanvs[TARGET_DC]critsuccess[doubles]critfail[doubles]
